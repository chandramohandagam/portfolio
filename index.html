Agro Mitra

Better Farming • Smarter Decisions • Higher Yields

An AI-powered agriculture platform connecting farmers, dealers, and logistics. Combines real-time mandi prices, soil-matched crop advisory, Dijkstra route optimization, and multi-language support.

Role: Frontend Developer & UI/UX Contributor Web & Android App (React + Vite)
1. The Problem

Farmers in rural areas face unpredictable market prices, lack of crop guidance, soil deterioration, and difficulty finding reliable buyers and transport services, leading to lower income and post-harvest crop loss.

2. Our Solution

A unified web and mobile platform connecting farmers, dealers, and transport. Combines real-time mandi data, Gromitra AI analysis, and Dijkstra shortest-path routing from sowing to selling.

  • Live Market Prices: Real-time crop prices across nearby regional mandis.
  • Crop & Seed Guidance: Personalized advice based on soil type and season.
  • Soil Health Analysis: N-P-K nutrient tracking & fertilizer dose cards.
  • Fleet & Delivery Management: Live vehicle tracking with OTP security.
  • AI Market Analysis: Gromitra AI price trend forecasting.
  • Multi-Language: English, Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi.
Frontend: React + Vite Tailwind CSS Framer Motion i18next Recharts
Backend: Node.js + Express MongoDB FastAPI Supabase Sync JWT
Cloud & AI: Groq AI Clerk Auth Cloudinary Razorpay Render

Diagnosphere

Early Detection | Smarter Decisions | Better Health

An AI-powered diagnostic platform empowering early disease prediction, symptom analysis, and secure health record telemetry to bridge healthcare access in underserved rural and semi-urban communities.

Role: Frontend Developer & UI Architecture HTML5 / CSS3 / JavaScript / Python Flask
1. The Problem

In rural and semi-urban areas, people often face delays in diagnosis due to lack of specialists, high consultation costs, and hospital distance. Diseases like diabetes and hypertension go undiagnosed until advanced stages.

2. Our Solution

Diagnosphere predicts potential diseases based on symptoms, analyzes medical history, and provides personalized wellness guidance with doctor consultation workflows to make healthcare accessible and accurate.

  • AI Disease Prediction: Predicts conditions from symptoms and user inputs.
  • Symptom Checker: Step-by-step diagnostic questionnaire flow.
  • Health Records: Store and manage patient vitals and lab reports securely.
  • Multi-Language: Accessible in English, Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada.
  • Doctor Consultation: Telemedicine workflows for specialist reviews.
  • Future Scope: Offline support, wearable sync, rural clinic expansion.
Frontend: HTML5 CSS3 Custom JavaScript (ES6+) React.js (Planned)
Backend: Python Flask Microframework REST APIs
Data & Cloud: MongoDB Database Cloudinary Media SSL Encryption

AI Smart Health Care

Smarter Diagnosis | Better Treatment | Healthier Tomorrow

An AI-powered healthcare platform that assists users with early disease prediction, personalized health guidance, medicine reminder alerts, emergency dispatch, and certified doctor teleconsultations.

Role: Frontend Architecture & API Integration Web & Android App (React / Tailwind / Flask)
1. The Problem

Rural and semi-urban patients face specialist shortages, high costs, and fragmented emergency care, leading to untreated chronic illness and medication non-adherence.

2. Our Solution

An integrated smart healthcare companion delivering early disease prediction, tailored wellness plans, dosage reminders, and immediate emergency hospital and ambulance dispatch.

  • Disease Prediction: ML-driven assessment from user symptoms.
  • Symptom Checker: Quick, clear preliminary wellness triage.
  • Medicine Reminder: Automated dosage schedules and notifications.
  • Emergency Assistance: Instant contact to hospitals and ambulance dispatch.
  • Doctor Consultation: Direct channel with certified physicians.
  • Multi-Language: English, Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada.
Frontend: HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript Tailwind CSS React.js
Backend: Python Flask MySQL REST APIs
Tools & DevOps: Git & GitHub VS Code Postman
